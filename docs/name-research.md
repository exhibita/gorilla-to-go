# Name research

Decision: **Gorilla To Go (GTG)**.

## Candidates

- Gorilla names: Pocket Gorilla, Thump, Opposable, Silverback, Knuckle Walker
- Vanilla names: Soft Serve, Two Scoops, Vanilla Bean, Sprinkles
- Mixed: Gorilla To Go, Monkey Business, Banana Hammock

## Checks (web search on 2026-10-17)

This was a first check only. It did not include the official trademark databases or domain availability.

| Name | Result | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Gorilla To Go | No app or product with this exact name. Closest match: the children's book "Go-Go Gorillas" (2010). | Low |
| GTG | Common abbreviation for "good to go". Cannot be protected as a brand. Use as a nickname only. | n/a |
| Pocket Gorilla | No app, product or company found. | Low |
| Thump | Mackie uses "Thump" for loudspeakers and a control app (with ™). Other apps use the word. | High |
